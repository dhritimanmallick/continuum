Privacy Policy

Vyuhaa Continuum (web and Android app) · Last updated 7 November 2026

Vyuhaa Continuum ("Continuum", "we") is provided by Vyuhaa Med Data Private Limited, 4/608 G1 VOC Street, Perungudi, OMR, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600041, India. Continuum helps mothers, families and hospital outpatient departments (OPDs) keep track of antenatal, postnatal, newborn and child care. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) health data management policy.

Not a medical device. Continuum is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. It organises care schedules, records and reminders. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call 108.

1. Information we collect

  • Identity and contact: name, age, sex, mobile number, preferred language, and your ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) number if you choose to link it.
  • Health information: pregnancy and postnatal details, visit notes, vital signs, test orders and results, scans, prescriptions, vaccinations, and your baby's records, as entered by you or by the hospital caring for you.
  • Family members you add to your Family Circle, with the access level you choose.
  • Documents and photos you or the hospital upload, such as paper prescriptions or lab reports.
  • Messages sent to you on WhatsApp by the hospital through Continuum, and your replies.
  • Hospital staff: name, role and work actions (who registered, ordered or sent what), kept as an audit trail.
  • Technical data: basic device and app information needed to run the service securely. We do not use advertising identifiers and do not show ads.

The app uses the camera only when you choose to photograph a document. It does not access your location unless you use "Nearby on Map" and allow it in your browser or phone.

2. Why we use it

  • To keep your care schedule and show what is due, such as tests, scans, visits and vaccinations.
  • To let the hospital you visit register you, see your history, order tests, return results and dispense medicines.
  • To send you reminders and updates on WhatsApp in your language, and labour updates to family members you choose.
  • To show doctors, labs and indicative costs across hospitals near you.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems, and keep an audit trail of who did what.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Consent and your control

  • Linking your ABHA and sharing records between hospitals happens only with your consent, which you can review or withdraw at any time under Hospital Consents.
  • You choose which family members can see your information, and how much.
  • You can ask the hospital or us to stop WhatsApp messages at any time.

4. Who we share it with

  • Hospitals, labs and pharmacies involved in your care, and only to the extent needed for that care or as you consent.
  • Service providers who process data on our instructions: cloud hosting; Google (Gemini) to read uploaded documents and summarise records for clinicians; a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (Gupshup) and Meta to deliver WhatsApp messages; Google Maps for nearby search.
  • ABDM health information exchange, when you link your ABHA and give consent.
  • Authorities where Indian law requires it.

5. AI features

AI is used to read documents into structured fields, write short summaries for clinicians and translate reminders. AI does not make diagnoses or decide your care. What is due is set by clinical rules approved by doctors, and clinicians review AI output before acting on it.

6. Storage, security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with access controls. Health records are kept for as long as needed for your care and as required by medical-records law. When they are no longer needed, they are deleted or anonymised.

7. Children's data

Records for newborns and children are created and managed by their parent or guardian, or by the treating hospital with the parent's consent. Continuum is not intended for use by children themselves.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or name someone to act for you. Write to dhritiman@vyuhaadata.com with the mobile number registered with Continuum. We respond within 30 days. Some records may need to be kept by the hospital under medical-records law. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Changes

We will post any change to this policy here and update the date above. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app.
